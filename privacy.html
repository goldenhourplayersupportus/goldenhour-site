Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

Golden Hour Entertainment ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

Information We Collect

  • Contact information you provide, such as your name, email address, and phone number.
  • Messages you send us through email, SMS, or messaging apps when contacting player support.
  • Basic usage data such as pages visited on this website.

How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your support requests and questions.
  • To send you account updates, promotions, and offers you opted in to receive.
  • To improve our services and website.

Text Messaging (SMS)

If you opt in to receive text messages from us, we collect your phone number and messaging consent. Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies. You may opt out at any time by replying STOP. For help, reply HELP or contact us at support@goldenhourplay.com.

All the above categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information won't be shared with any third parties.

Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only as needed to operate our services (for example, with messaging providers that deliver our emails and texts), or when required by law.

Your Choices

  • You may opt out of promotional emails using the unsubscribe link in any email.
  • You may opt out of text messages by replying STOP at any time.
  • You may contact us at support@goldenhourplay.com to request access to or deletion of your information.
